01

What this app does

Yo Soy Oldri is an independent urban music artist. His short-form videos — daily music Shorts and visual comedies set to his own songs — are the core of his channel. The Yo Soy Oldri app is registered for one narrow job: after the artist authorizes it with his own TikTok account, it will publish his finished, original videos to that same account through TikTok's official Content Posting API (Direct Post), and report back the publishing status of each post. The app is currently pending TikTok's review; public Direct Post publishing will become available only after TikTok approves it. Until then, API posts are limited to private visibility under TikTok's rules for unapproved apps. It is operated by the artist himself for his own catalog. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it never posts to anyone else's account.

02

How it works

Once TikTok approves the app for public Direct Post, every post will follow the same four steps through TikTok's official authorization and posting flow. Before approval, the same flow can be tested only with private posts.

1

Authorize

The artist signs in on TikTok's own page through Login Kit and grants the app its three permissions. TikTok — never this app — handles the password.

2

Prepare

A finished video from the artist's own catalog is selected, with its title and description written by the artist's team.

3

Direct Post

After TikTok approves the app, the video is sent to the authorized account through the Content Posting API, with the AI-generated-content disclosure applied where the video requires it.

4

Verify

The app checks the publishing status TikTok returns and, after approval, confirms the public post is live on @yo.soy.oldri before the next video is queued.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Yo Soy Oldri app handles, why, and what choices the account holder has. In one sentence: the app handles the minimum data TikTok's API requires to publish the artist's own videos to his own account — and nothing about anyone else.

Data operator: Yo Soy Oldri (the artist and developer organization of the same name)

App: “Yo Soy Oldri” — Web / Desktop application registered on the TikTok Developer Portal

Contact: Direct message to @yo.soy.oldri on TikTok

1.Information the app receives

When the account holder authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides the app with:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID, username, display name, and avatar image — used only to confirm the app is connected to the artist's own account, @yo.soy.oldri.
  • An access token issued by TikTok that lets the app act on that authorization. The app never sees the account password.
  • Content supplied by the operator: the artist's own video files and the titles, descriptions, and disclosure settings prepared for each post.
  • Publishing results: the post identifiers and status responses TikTok returns for each upload, used to confirm a video published successfully.

This website itself is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects no information from its visitors.

2.What the app does not collect

  • No passwords or sign-in credentials of any kind;
  • No information about followers, viewers, commenters, or any other TikTok user;
  • No direct messages, contacts, browsing history, or device identifiers;
  • No payment information — the app sells nothing and charges nothing;
  • No data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties, in any form.

3.How information is used

Information is used for exactly three purposes: (a) confirming the app is connected to the correct account; (b) uploading and publishing the artist's videos through the Content Posting API, including the AI-generated-content disclosure where a video requires it; and (c) verifying and recording that each post published successfully. It is used for no other purpose.

4.Sharing

Data flows only between the app and TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs — video content and post metadata are sent to TikTok to be published, and TikTok returns status information. No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

5.Retention and deletion

The access token is retained only while the authorization is active. Publishing records (which video was posted, when, and TikTok's response) are kept as part of the artist's own release records. When authorization is revoked, or on request by direct message to @yo.soy.oldri, the app's stored tokens and TikTok-derived profile data are deleted.

6.Security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored with access restricted to the operator, transmitted only over encrypted connections, and never published or embedded in this website. No security method is perfect, but the app's narrow data scope means it holds very little that could be exposed.

7.The account holder's choices

  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings → Security → Apps and websites), which immediately ends the app's ability to publish;
  • Request deletion of stored data at any time by direct message to @yo.soy.oldri;
  • Review granted permissions on the same TikTok settings page at any time.

8.Children

The app is an internal publishing tool for a professional music account. It is not directed at children, offers no accounts or sign-up to the public, and knowingly collects no information from anyone under 18.

9.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes in how TikTok data is handled will not take effect for previously authorized use until the account holder has had the opportunity to review them and, where TikTok requires, re-authorize.

10.Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent by direct message to the artist's official TikTok account, @yo.soy.oldri.
